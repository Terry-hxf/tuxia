Get In Touch

We value your feedback and want to hear from you. Whether you have found a bug, have a suggestion for a new tool, or want to report an issue with the website, please reach out. Your input helps us improve TUXIA for everyone.

Feedback and Bug Reports

If you encounter a problem with any of the tools — such as a file format that does not work as expected, a browser compatibility issue, or a visual bug — please let us know. When reporting a bug, include as much detail as possible:

  • Which tool you were using
  • The type of image file (format, approximate size)
  • Your browser and operating system
  • What you expected to happen vs. what actually happened

Send your feedback and bug reports to: tuxia.feedback@outlook.com

We typically respond within 2 to 3 business days.

Feature Requests

Have an idea for a new image tool or an improvement to an existing one? We would love to hear about it. Common feature requests we consider include:

  • New output formats or export options
  • Additional customization settings for tools
  • New tool suggestions for image workflows
  • Usability improvements

While we cannot implement every suggestion, we carefully review all submissions and prioritize features that benefit the most users.

Frequently Asked Questions

Before reaching out, you may find answers to common questions on our Privacy Policy page (which explains how TUXIA handles data) or our Terms of Use page (which covers usage guidelines). Each tool also has its own FAQ section at the bottom of its page.

Response Time

We are a small team building and maintaining TUXIA. We read every message and aim to respond within 2 to 3 business days. Bug reports that include reproduction steps and screenshots help us respond faster and more effectively.

Collaboration and Partnerships

For inquiries about link exchanges, advertising partnerships (beyond Google AdSense), or other collaboration opportunities, please contact us at the email address above with “Collaboration” in the subject line.
